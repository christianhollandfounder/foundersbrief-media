# Evening trial reels for christianhollandd, 17 Nov 2026 (v2)

Post these by hand in the Instagram app on your personal account with Trial turned on. Buffer cannot post trial reels. Times are ET, every 30 minutes from 5:00 PM. If a slot has already passed, post the next one at its time and fit the missed one into a later gap.

These v2 cuts (`e01_fp.mp4` to `e10_fp.mp4`) replace the first evening pack. They blend Daniel Dalen's clips page with the Founders Podcast Instagram: a quote-led hook in big white type from frame one, a cut on the music beat about every second, new on-screen words on every cut, timed to what Sebastian is saying, b-roll that shows the line, and an ending that loops back into the first shot. Every hook and on-screen line is Sebastian's own words from FoundersBrief Films 001. The cuts differ from the reels on the FoundersBrief Films queue today.

Do not tag or collaborate with Sebastian or anyone at Mbodi on these, in the caption, on the reel, or in a story. No hashtags. Nothing here is for LinkedIn.

If a trial passes about 1,000 views, make it live on your grid.

Specs: 1080x1920, H.264 High, constant 23.976 fps, about 10 Mbps, keyframe every 2 seconds, AAC 192k. Sebastian's voice is the film's dialogue; the music bed is Vastness by Andrew Ev (Mixkit license).
